What this can and cannot do
It turns the averages you enter into probabilities using a Poisson goal model with a small correction for low scores. It does not know about injuries, line-ups, red cards, weather, motivation or how fixtures were scheduled, and small samples of matches give shaky averages. Treat the output as an estimate, not a forecast you can rely on.
